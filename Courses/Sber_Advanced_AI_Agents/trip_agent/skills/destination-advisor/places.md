# Reference destinations

- **Lisbon, Portugal** — coastal city break, mild winters, good for food/wine and
  a relaxed pace; budget-friendly for Western Europe.
- **Kyoto, Japan** — temples, gardens, traditional culture; best in spring
  (cherry blossoms) or autumn (foliage); mid-to-high budget.
- **Tbilisi, Georgia** — mountains, wine region day trips, very budget-friendly;
  good for a mix of nature and city life.
- **Marrakech, Morocco** — markets, desert day trips, warm most of the year;
  budget-to-mid, vivid and busy.
- **Reykjavik, Iceland** — nature/adventure (glaciers, hot springs, northern
  lights in winter); higher budget.
- **Bali, Indonesia** — beaches, surfing, wellness retreats; warm year-round;
  budget-to-mid.
- **Buenos Aires, Argentina** — tango, steak, nightlife, walkable
  neighborhoods; budget-friendly for the region.
- **Prague, Czech Republic** — historic old town, beer culture, easy to get
  around on foot; budget-friendly for Western/Central Europe.
